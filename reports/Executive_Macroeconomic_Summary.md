## 🎯 1. Key Business Insights
* **The Global Economic Gap:** Our world map visualization clearly highlights that global wealth is heavily concentrated in a few major nations. Superpowers like the US and China dominate total GDP volume, leaving a wide gap compared to developing regions.
* **Growth Speed vs. Size:** By exploring the scatter plots, we can see that smaller or middle-income countries often experience much higher year-over-year growth percentage spikes compared to large, stabilized economies. 
* **Historical Resiliency:** Using the dashboard timeline filters to look at historical economic downturns (like the 2008 financial crisis), we can easily spot which countries recovered quickly and which ones suffered long-term stagnation.

---

## 🧹 2. Data Cleaning & Preparation Summary
To make sure our charts and dashboard metrics were completely accurate for this report, the raw dataset went through a standard data preparation process:
1. **Removing Non-Country Groupings:** Removed rows that represented global totals or banking aggregates (like "World" or "Low & middle income") so they wouldn't mess up individual country comparisons.
2. **Standardizing Names:** Cleaned up messy country names (e.g., changing "Bahamas, The" to "The Bahamas") to ensure smooth dropdown filtering.
3. **Calculating Growth Rates:** Formatted the numeric columns and created a dedicated calculation to track annual GDP growth percentages.

---

## 🧭 3. Analyst Recommendations
1. **Look Beyond Total GDP Size:** Investors and businesses shouldn't just target the largest countries. Smaller nations showing high, steady growth rates often offer better untapped market opportunities.
2. **Analyze Crisis Performance:** Before expanding into a new regional market, use the dashboard's timeline slider to evaluate how that country performed during past global recessions to gauge its market stability.
